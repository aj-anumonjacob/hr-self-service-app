# Employee Attendance and Monitoring Policy
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## 1. Objective
This policy defines the expectations, rules, and procedures regarding recording working hours using the Standalone Employee Attendance Application.

## 2. Workplace Geofencing
Employees are required to record their Check-In and Check-Out events within their assigned work locations. Geofences are configured dynamically to establish validity of presence.

## 3. Disciplinary Safeguards
Technical failures (e.g. GPS drift, lack of network, device battery failure) will not result in automatic salary deduction or disciplinary action. Manual corrections can be requested by employees and approved by managers.
