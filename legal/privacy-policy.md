# General Application Privacy Policy
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how the Company collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained from employees using the Antigravity Attendance Application.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect:
- Account identifiers (Employee Number, Full Name, Email, Mobile number)
- Device identification (Device UUID, Model, OS Version)
- Work location metrics (Precise GPS coordinates at time of Check-In/Check-Out)
- Authentication credentials (hashed password)

## 3. Storage and Security
All personal data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and at rest within secure database systems. We do not sell or lease employee data to third parties.
