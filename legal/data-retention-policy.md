# Data Retention Policy
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## Retention Schedules
- **Attendance Records**: 5 years (or as required by local labor laws).
- **Check-in/out Coordinates**: 90 days.
- **Background Location Logs**: 30 days.
- **Biometric Templates**: Deleted immediately upon employee deactivation or consent withdrawal.
- **Offline Logs & Audit trails**: 1 year.
