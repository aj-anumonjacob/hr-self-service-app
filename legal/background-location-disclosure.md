# Prominent Background Location Disclosure (In-App)
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## Disclosure Content
**Location Verification During Active Duty**
This application collects location data to enable geofence verification and movement tracking during your active work sessions, even when the app is closed or not in use.

## Core Purpose
Verifying employee presence and authorized movement during an active checked-in work session.

## Data Governance
- Location services will stop immediately upon tapping "Check Out" or initiating a break.
- Data is securely queued locally while offline and uploaded once a connection is restored.
- Coordinates are subject to automatic retention rules (e.g. deleted after 90 days).
