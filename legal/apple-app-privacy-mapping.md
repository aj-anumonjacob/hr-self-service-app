## Labels Declared

- **Contact Info**: Name, Email Address, Phone Number (Account identification).
- **User Content**: Photos (Consent-based biometric verification).
- **Identifiers**: Device ID (Device binding security).
- **Diagnostics**: Crash Data (Stability improvements).
