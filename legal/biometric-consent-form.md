# Biometric Data Notice and Consent Form
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## Explicit Biometric Consent
By toggling "Agree" during enrollment:
1. I consent to the capture of my facial landmarks to create a template (embedding) for identity matching.
2. I understand that the template is encrypted and stored securely on my device (and server backup).
3. I understand that my raw face photographs are deleted immediately after template generation.
4. I acknowledge that I can request a revocation of my biometric enrollment or select an approved exception from my manager.
