# Biometric Data Notice
*Draft for review by qualified legal and privacy professionals.*

## 1. Purpose of Biometric Verification
Face verification is used as an optional or mandatory policy to prevent buddy punching and confirm the identity of the employee checking in.

## 2. Biometric Data Collection
The system utilizes on-device Google ML Kit and TensorFlow Lite models to generate a face template (embedding). We do not collect or store raw photographs of employee faces on our servers.
